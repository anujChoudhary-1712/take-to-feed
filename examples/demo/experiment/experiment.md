# Signal Desk cover experiment

**Hypothesis:** A question grounded in the demo timestamp will encourage more saves per account reached than an outcome-led cover.

**Test:** Compare the shipped cover A and hook variant B from the [hook sheet](../hooks.md). For this fictional Instagram test, only the cover changes. The remaining slides and caption are held constant. Use the prior timestamped workflow carousel as the account baseline. The variants run on different days and reach different organic audiences.

**Primary metric:** Saves divided by reach at 72 hours. **Decision rule:** Repeat a promising angle when distribution differs materially before adopting it as the default.

| Post | Saves | Reach | Saves / reach |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Comparable baseline | 11 | 580 | 1.90% |
| A | 12 | 600 | 2.00% |
| B | 8 | 250 | 3.20% |

**Result:** Inconclusive. B has the higher observed rate and fewer total saves on substantially lower reach. The comparison cannot separate the cover from audience and distribution differences.

**Next test:** Keep A as the current default. Repeat the outcome-versus-question comparison on another timestamped workflow with the same observation window and comparable posting slots.
