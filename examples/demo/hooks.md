# Hook review for Signal Desk

**Audience:** Podcast producers who leave timestamped edit notes.

**Payoff:** Show where a note lives, how to assign it, and what it looks like when resolved.

**Evidence:** [Demo notes](transcript.md) and the three source screenshots.

Scores are clarity / audience relevance / specificity / payoff on the skill's 0–2 scale.

| Angle | Hook | First visual or next beat | Evidence | Scores |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outcome | Give every edit note a home. | Show the note at 12:40 in the episode review. | Episode, assignment, and resolved states | 2 / 2 / 1 / 2 |
| Specific question | Who owns the cut at 12:40? | Open on the note, then show Leah's assignment. | Episode and assignment states | 2 / 2 / 2 / 2 |
| Familiar problem | Your edit note needs more than a timestamp. | Show the timestamp, then reveal the owner and status. | Assignment and resolved states | 2 / 2 / 1 / 2 |

**Selected:** “Give every edit note a home.” It covers all three steps and fits both the [carousel cover](exports/01.jpg) and the [reel opening](reel/reel.mp4). The question scores higher for specificity and remains the test variant. It narrows the story to assignment, so use it when the audience already understands why the note needs an owner.
