# Content Experiments

Follow the [experiment guide](references/experiment-guide.md) and copy the [experiment log](assets/starter/experiment.md) into the user's local content workspace.

1. Choose the decision the test should inform. Write one hypothesis and identify the single intended change between versions.
2. Choose the primary metric, denominator, measurement window, and comparison before publishing. Use the account's available analytics and comparable baseline posts.
3. Record each version, publish time, and anything that could affect distribution. Use account data supplied by the user or available through an authorized analytics tool.
4. Report raw counts with rates and record the next decision. If the data is missing, the sample is thin, or distribution differs materially, capture the limitation and recommend a repeat or mark the result inconclusive.
